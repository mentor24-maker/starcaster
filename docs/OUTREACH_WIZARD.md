# Outreach — the one-page guide

**Where:** Engage › **Outreach** (first item under Engage).

Outreach is the one screen for finding conversations on YouTube, Substack and
Reddit and replying to them. You no longer need to visit each channel's own
screen to do the day's work.

## The tabs across the top

- **All channels** — what is waiting today on every channel at once.
- **YouTube**, **Substack**, **Reddit** — the same, for one channel, plus that
  channel's five stages listed underneath.

## The five stages (one channel at a time)

Each stage shows a plain sentence saying where it stands, and a label:
*Done*, *Needs you*, *Waiting for the Mini*, *Not started*, or *Cannot tell*
(a reading failed; the sentence says why — it is never shown as done).

1. **Connect** — the Mac Mini's browser is signed in to the site, and you have
   chosen which account it posts as. Nothing posts until you choose.
2. **Find creators** — the channels (YouTube) or subreddits (Reddit) worth
   watching. You approve or reject each one.
3. **Find conversations** — new posts and videos from those creators that are
   worth a reply.
4. **Write replies** — drafted replies waiting for you to approve or reject.
5. **Learn** — the lessons the drafts are written with, learned from what you
   approved and rejected. Switch lessons on or off, or add your own.

The first stage that needs something opens on its own; click any stage's
heading to open or close it.

## Today and Start

At the top of every tab is the **Today** line, for example
*"3 replies waiting for you · 12 new conversations · Mini signed in"*.

**Start** walks you through the waiting replies one after another. For each
one you see what it replies to and up to three drafts. Keys while you walk:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **1**, **2**, **3** | choose that draft |
| **E** | edit the chosen draft |
| **A** | approve it |
| **R** | reject it (you are asked why, so the drafts get better) |
| **→** (right arrow) | skip it for now |

Typing inside the edit box types — the keys only act outside it.
**Nothing posts from here.** Approve hands the reply to the Mini, which posts
it at the channel's normal pace, exactly as the channel's own screen does.

## Where the old screens went

The separate **YouTube**, **Substack** and **Reddit** screens are still there,
under the **Advanced** heading at the bottom of the Engage menu, for detail
work Outreach does not cover. Old links and bookmarks to them still open them
(for example `#page=engageCommentResponsesPage` is the Reddit screen). A
decision made on either screen is the same decision — they read and write the
same lists.
