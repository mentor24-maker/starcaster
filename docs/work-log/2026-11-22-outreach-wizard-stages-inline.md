## 2026-11-22 — Do every outreach stage inside the Outreach screen, with a first-run path for a new channel (#1092)

The Outreach screen (Engage › Outreach) used to send you to other screens for some of its steps. Some stages had a button that jumped to the Substack Miner, or to a channel's old Engage page. Now every step happens on the Outreach screen itself, and nothing on it leads away.

What changed:

- Substack's "Find creators" now lists the Substack writers right inside the stage. You can paste a writer's link, search for writers on your keywords, and approve or reject each one. It uses the Substack Miner's own buttons behind the scenes, so a decision made here is the same decision as one made there.
- "Write replies" now has a button that walks you through the waiting replies one at a time, and another that writes drafts for ideas still waiting to be written.
- A brand-new channel shows "Getting started": five steps from signing the Mini in to approving your first reply. Each step says where it stands, what happens next, and roughly how long that takes.

Why it matters: outreach can now be run from start to finish on one screen, and a new channel has a clear path to its first reply.

One thing the ticket asked for does not exist yet: keywords suggested from the canon. The keyword step starts from your saved keywords instead, or the Substack Miner's when none are saved, and it says so.
