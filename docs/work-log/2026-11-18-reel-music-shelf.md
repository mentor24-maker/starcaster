## 2026-11-18 — Pick royalty-free background music for each video (Reel Spinner 5a) (#1058)

Reel Spinner can now choose a background track for every video it edits, so
nobody has to pick music by hand.

Thirty tracks by Kevin MacLeod, five for each mood the scoring step assigns
(hopeful, urgent, reflective, determined, playful, calm), are now in the
Studio › Music folder on Google Drive. His licence allows them in YouTube
videos for free as long as the description credits him, and his music does
not trigger YouTube's automatic copyright claims. A committed list records
each track's licence, credit line, mood and length.

The picker matches a track to each video's mood and length. It never gives two
videos cut from the same recording the same track in a row. When it has
nothing for a mood, it uses the calmest track and says so. The rough-cut
screen will show the chosen track's name once that screen is built (SC-835).
